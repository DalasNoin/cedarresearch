CEDAR

Communicating Evidence and Demonstrations of AI Risk

CEDAR is a research organisation making the trajectory of AI legible. We produce original research and technical demonstrations of what AI systems can already do, and translate the evidence for the public and for decision makers. Our current focus is mass deanonymisation and AI-enabled surveillance.
