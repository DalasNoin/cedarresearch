Cedar works on communicating the risks of AI, from mass surveillance to the catastrophic threats posed by advanced AI, through research demonstrations and human studies.

Simon Lermen

Founder

Simon researches what AI systems can already do to people, and works to make those capabilities legible to the public and to policymakers before they are deployed at scale.

He is the author of Large-Scale Online Deanonymization With LLMs, which showed that language models can identify people at scale from ordinary public writing. The work was covered by the Guardian and the New York Times.

He is a researcher at MATS and is based in London.
